How to use {{ APP_NAME }}

1. Creating Activities

  1. Navigate to the Activities page.
  2. Click New Activity and enter a name.
  3. For each activity, click Sub to add sub-activities.
  4. Set the name, scheduled time, difficulty, base EXP, and attribute weights.
Notes
  • Sub-activity names should be unique within an activity.
  • Attribute weights should sum to 1.0.
  • Difficulty multipliers affect both EXP rewards and penalties.

2. Scheduling Tasks

  1. Go to the Timetable page.
  2. Use the date picker to select a date.
  3. Click + New Day to create a timetable, then Add Task.
  4. Choose an activity and sub-activity, set start time and duration.
  5. For recurring tasks, check Repeats weekly and select weekdays.
Notes
  • Avoid overlapping tasks.
  • Account for buffer time between tasks.

3. Logging Completion

  1. On the Dashboard, find your task.
  2. Click Complete, Partial, or Skip.
  3. Fill in the required fields and submit.
Notes
  • Log tasks as soon as possible for accurate tracking.
  • There is a 15-minute grace period.
  • Partial completions and skips affect your discipline factor.

4. Finalizing the Day

  1. At the end of your day, review all tasks on the Dashboard.
  2. Ensure all tasks are marked as completed, partial, or skipped.
  3. Click Finalize Day.
Notes
  • Once finalized, you cannot change task statuses for that day.
  • Finalizing calculates your daily EXP and updates level progress.

Consistency is key. Regular task completion and honest logging will help you grow over time.
