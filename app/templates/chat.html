AI Chat needs your consent

To reply sensibly, your AI companion is sent a slice of your own data with every message — the most recent slice of:

  • Recent activity — tasks completed, skipped, or partially done in the last 14 days, including judge verdicts.
  • Journal entries — the latest ones you have written.
  • Today's summary — today's completions, XP, streak and level.

This context is not stored — it is rebuilt fresh for each message and only sent to your configured LLM provider (OmniRoute). You can revoke consent at any time from your profile or the chat page. Revoking stops sending but keeps your entire chat history.
